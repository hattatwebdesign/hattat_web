    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Question not answered here? Just drop us a line. Contact →

    How much does a small business website cost?

    It depends on the scope: a one-page website costs far less than an online shop. After a free first call you receive a clear written quote with no hidden costs. On a tight budget we start with the essentials and expand later.

    How long until my website is live?

    A one-page website is often ready within a few weeks; larger websites or shops take longer. We agree the exact timeline together.

    Do you work with businesses in the UK, Switzerland and Türkiye?

    Yes. We work with SMEs in the UK, Switzerland, Türkiye and Germany. Meetings take place by video call, phone or email – in English, Turkish or German.

    Will my website be found on Google?

    Every website is built to be technically clean and search-engine friendly. We choose keywords your customers really use and, on request, set up your Google Business Profile for local search.

    Can I edit the website myself later?

    Yes, if you like. We set things up so you can change text, images or offers yourself – or we look after it for you.

    Can my website be in more than one language?

    Yes. We build websites in English, Turkish and German – also combined, with each language found separately on Google.
